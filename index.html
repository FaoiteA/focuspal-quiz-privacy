FocusPal Quiz Battle – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

FocusPal Quiz Battle ("the app") is made by FaoiteApps, an independent developer in Ireland. This policy explains how the app handles your information.

Information we collect

The app does not collect, store on our servers, or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no analytics, no tracking and no advertising.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress (XP, level, wins, chosen buddy, cleared countries and whether you have unlocked the full game) is saved only on your device. It is deleted if you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Region

The app reads your phone's region setting, on your device only, to choose which World Tour country pack is free. This is never sent anywhere.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment details. See Google's privacy policy for how Google handles purchase data.

Reporting a question

If you tap "Report question", the app opens your own email app with a pre-filled message. Nothing is sent unless you choose to send it. If you do, we receive your email address and message and use them only to fix the question and reply to you.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
